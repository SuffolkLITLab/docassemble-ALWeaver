### Bug 3 (High): Standalone `ALAddress` fails on `{{ property_address.address }}` in DOCX, fails on all dotted labels in PDFs, and swallows unsupported methods in DOCX

     * Then [`apply_object_types`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/docassemble/ALWeaver/interview_generator.py#L3758-L3761) checks `display[len(name) + 1 :] in supported` where `display = field.final_display_var or field.variable`. Because `field.final_display_var` was never set before `apply_object_types`, `display` is `"property_address.address.address"`, its suffix `"address.address"` is not in `supported`, and `{{ property_address.address }}` is left as a broken custom field (`property_address.address.address`) despite being listed as supported in [`variable-support.md`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/variable-support.md#L50).
  2. **PDF dotted labels (`property_address.city`, `property_address.block()`) fail**:
     * Both [`apply_object_types`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/docassemble/ALWeaver/interview_generator.py#L3718) (*"Dotted labels are accepted in PDF and DOCX"*) and [`variable-support.md`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/variable-support.md#L52) (*"Dotted PDF expressions also work"*) claim dotted PDF field names work for declared `ALAddress` objects.
     * However, [`DAField.fill_in_pdf_attributes`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/docassemble/ALWeaver/interview_generator.py#L1264) runs `varname(name_for_variable)`, which strips `.` and `()`, turning a PDF field named `property_address.city` into `field.variable = "property_addresscity"` and `field.final_display_var = "property_addresscity"`.   3. **DOCX unsupported method calls (`{{ property_address.unsupported_method() }}`) are silently rewritten to `property_address.block()`**:
     * [`_settable_docx_variable`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/docassemble/ALWeaver/interview_generator.py#L6733) strips any `.method()` call (`re.sub(r"\.\w+\(.*?\)$", "", possible_var)`), turning `property_address.geolocate()` into `field.variable = "property_address"`.
     * [`apply_object_types`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/docassemble/ALWeaver/interview_generator.py#L3744-L3749) sees `display == name` (`"property_address"`), rewrites `field.final_display_var = "property_address.block()"`, and marks it `DAFieldGroup.BUILT_IN`, contradicting [`variable-support.md`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/variable-support.md#L56) (*"Unsupported attributes still need authored questions"*).

## 3. Documented Release Limitations vs. Regressions

* **Documented / Intentional Scope Boundaries (Not Regressions)**:

## 4. Untested Risks

1. **`service_address` / `service_method` on custom person lists or with `copy_baseline_questions=True`**:
   * In `docassemble.AssemblyLine`'s `ql_baseline.yml`, `service_address` and `service_method` are defined **only** for `other_parties[i]` and `users[i]`—there is no `generic object: ALPeopleList` or `ALIndividual` block for `x[i].service_address` or `x[i].service_method`.
   * However, Weaver's [`is_reserved_label`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/docassemble/ALWeaver/interview_generator.py#L6490) and [`RESERVED_PERSON_ATTS_MAP`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/docassemble/ALWeaver/generator_constants.py#L316-L326) mark `_service_address` and `_service_method` as `DAFieldGroup.BUILT_IN` for **any** person prefix (e.g., `witnesses1_service_address`, `attorneys1_service_method`), and [`question_library.py`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/docassemble/ALWeaver/question_library.py#L58-L68) (`_ATTRIBUTE_KINDS`) has no templates for `service_address` or `service_method`. If a template uses `attorneys1_service_address` or `witnesses1_service_method`, Weaver suppresses custom question generation while AssemblyLine has no baseline question to satisfy `attorneys[0].service_address.address`, causing an undefined-variable error at runtime.
2. **Author-written custom object expressions alongside `apply_object_types`**:
   * While [`api_editor.py`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/docassemble/ALWeaver/api_editor.py#L10103-L10154) and [`question_library.py`](file:///home/quinten/docassemble-ALWeaver/docassemble/ALWeaver/question_library.py#L306-L333) carefully preserve author-written `.using(...)` declarations in existing YAML files, there is no test covering `generate_interview_from_path` when `interview_overrides={"object_types": {"property_address": "ALAddress"}}` is combined with ordinary custom fields on the same form (which would have caught **Bug 1**'s duplicate custom screens and stale `DAQuestion` block).
